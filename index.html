Tasks

Every task, including the ones ducks are hiding. Getting a task happens in two steps, and there is a column for each: Drawn in is its chance of being the next one pulled off the pile into the pond, which is what weight controls. Caught next is its chance of being the next one a duck actually hands you. A dash means the step does not apply yet.
